# Study history and scope

The earlier preprint, [Risk Averse Alert Prioritization for IDS Using Subnormal
Gaussian Fuzzy Models](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.27299), introduced the
SGFN representation and scalar score. This manuscript evaluates existing scores
with matched inputs and makes no claim to introduce that score again.

An audit of an unpublished comparison identified unequal score inputs, use of
held-out outcomes in configuration selection, incompatible native scales, and
insufficient reporting of critical-alert deferral. The present comparison uses
common inputs, isolated fitting and selection, and separate interval-level and
alert-level outcomes. Online Resource 1, Section S2 documents the distinction.

The frozen primary protocol and six primary paired tests remain separate from
the dated exploratory amendments. Chronological forward evaluation, support
diagnostics, policy sensitivity, randomized ties, and grouping are explicitly
identified in their specifications. Null-test non-rejection is not equivalence.

This repository preserves scientific specifications, source, tests, and numerical
evidence needed to inspect those decisions. Private editorial discussions and
superseded manuscript drafts are not part of the public research artifact.
